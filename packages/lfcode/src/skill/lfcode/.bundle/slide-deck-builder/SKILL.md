# Slide Deck Builder

Build a coherent deck whose narrative and visual output both survive review.

## Workflow

1. Identify source decks, template or theme, audience, duration, aspect ratio, required slides, assets, and output format.
2. Inspect layouts, masters, fonts, notes, charts, links, media, and existing slide dimensions. Reuse established components and styles where possible.
3. Outline the narrative before editing. Keep claims, source notes, speaker guidance, and open questions distinguishable; do not invent data or citations.
4. Create or modify slides with consistent hierarchy, readable text, aligned objects, meaningful alt text when supported, and sensible fallback for missing assets.
5. Render the full deck and inspect title, dense, chart, image, transition, and final slides. Check for overflow, clipping, missing fonts, contrast, broken links, and unwanted blank slides.
6. Report the output path, narrative changes, render checks, sources, and items requiring human review.

## Boundaries

- Do not overwrite the only source deck or fetch copyrighted or external assets without authorization.
- Do not treat a successfully generated presentation as visually validated until it has been rendered or structurally checked.
- Keep secrets and personal data out of slide notes, screenshots, logs, and examples.
